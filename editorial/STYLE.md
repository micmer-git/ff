# FF newsletter voice and quality gate

Write in Italian with real accents. Read the latest three published issues in
full before drafting; inspect the latest five RSS enclosure covers before any
cover generation. The live publication is https://fortissimo.substack.com/ .
`newsletter_data.json` is a useful historical corpus, not proof of the latest
issue number. Verify numbering against both published posts and existing drafts.

The historical corpus uses an evocative, playful title, a compact Panorama and
numbered `ff.N.X` sections. Match the current issues rather than rigidly enforcing
an old template. Prefer surprising concrete observations and connections between
technology, nature and human life. Explain why each item matters in Michele's
voice. Avoid a generic news digest, corporate introductions, invented personal
experiences, repetitive endings and formulaic "non è X, è Y" contrasts.

Choose a coherent thread from the user's actual selections. Three to five strong
sections are a starting point, not a quota. Do not pad a thin week.
The issue should develop one recognizable question or theme through the sections;
do not merely connect unrelated news with transition sentences. Prefer three
strong connected items to four unrelated ones, returning unused sources to backlog.
Use horizontal separators between numbered sections and short bullet lists when
they sharpen a comparison or sequence. Alternate Michele's compact commentary
with original source material: supported X embeds, useful screenshots, figures
and brief attributed excerpts. Verify embedded media actually render in preview;
a bare link or empty card does not satisfy the visual intent. Do not replace all
source material with generated illustrations or generic charts. Generated cover
art should express the issue's central idea and stay separate from evidence.

Distinguish
quotation, source claim and editorial interpretation. Link facts where they occur.
Recheck numbers, dates, quotations and unstable claims against original sources.
An AI demo is not a benchmark; a simulation is not realized wealth; a clinical
case report is not evidence of treatment efficacy; progression-free survival is
not overall survival. Verify that a linked paper supports the adjacent claim.

Images must explain something or establish the issue's visual identity. Preserve
source, credit, reuse basis, caption and descriptive alt text. A screenshot is a
lead: read axes, units, sample, date and footnotes; verify the original. Do not
turn a forecast or counterfactual line into a measurement. Do not expose private
Discord UI or personal details in an illustration. Use an original diagram with
verified data if reuse rights are unclear. Generated art is illustrative, never
evidence. Use available image-generation skills only after references are viewed.
For the established mixed cover style: three simple raw SVG-like vignettes and
a background derived from the dominant idea; no generic glossy AI collage.

Metadata: editorial title and concrete subtitle; short ASCII slug; SEO title
under 60 characters where practical; SEO description 50–160 characters; one
social description; at most three meaningful tags. No keyword stuffing.

Final gate: reread the entire draft, verify each claim and link, preview figures
and captions, check saved metadata, and reopen the draft to verify persistence.
Report remaining uncertainties honestly. Leave it unpublished.
